Reading the page… this takes a few seconds.

For a clean read

  • Get all four corner squares in the frame — they tell the reader which month it is and where the grid sits.
  • Lay the page as flat as you can. A gentle curl is fine; a folded corner is not.
  • Even light beats bright light. Avoid a hard shadow across the middle of the page.
  • Fill boxes with a dark pen. Ticks, crosses and scribbles all read as yes; an empty box means no.
